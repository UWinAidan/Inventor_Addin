AWB title block: revised for approval

One title block, 180 by 50 mm, the same on every sheet size (ISO A4 to A0, ANSI A to E). White sheet, third angle projection. Every value is read from the model or the drawing, so the print changes when they do. All values below are made up. Revised after Aidan's answers A to G (2026-11-20): the logo panel a third narrower (30 to 20 mm), the tolerance box 45 to 30 mm wide, the description and the rows under it 25 mm wider, part number and revision 7 mm.

Metric sheet, drawn at about 7 times full size. Yellow numbers match the table below. Labels 1.8 mm, values 2.8 mm, description 4 mm, part number and revision 7 mm bold, status 2.5 mm so "WORK IN PROGRESS" fits. Text in Tahoma (Inventor's drawing font).

Imperial sheet

Only the tolerance box (2), the units, the weight's unit and the size differ. The imperial wording wraps onto three lines to fit the 30 mm box.

On the sheet

A3 landscape. Border 20 mm on the left (filing edge), 10 mm elsewhere. The finish notes block sits on the title block; the views are placeholders.
A4 portrait: the 180 mm block fills the width inside the border exactly. ANSI A portrait has 190 mm inside its 0.5 in border, so it fits there too.
